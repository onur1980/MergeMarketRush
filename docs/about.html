Merge Market Rush

Merge Market Rush is a market puzzle game where you merge matching goods, serve customer orders, clear short shifts, and grow a tiny shop through levels, collections, Daily Order, and Endless Rush.

Version: 1.0.0 (23)

Developer: Onur Akçakavak

Support: onuragames@gmail.com

Credits

Game design, development, art direction, and publishing by Onur Akçakavak.

Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

The game does not require a separate in-app account and does not use an Onur-operated gameplay server or chat backend. Progress and settings are stored on your device, with platform services used where available.

Ad serving, where enabled, uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and may also use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation with participating ad networks such as Unity Ads when that runtime is configured. Consent and privacy choices, where required, use Google User Messaging Platform.

In-app purchases, where enabled, use Google Play Billing. The current purchase flow is for the optional non-consumable No Ads entitlement.
