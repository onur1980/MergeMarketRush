Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Merge Market Rush ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is an Android merge-and-serve market puzzle game developed by Onur Akçakavak. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play the App, use ads or purchases, change privacy choices, or contact support.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android version of Merge Market Rush distributed through Google Play. The game does not require a separate in-app account and does not use an Onur-operated gameplay server, player profile database, chat system, leaderboard server, or cloud backend.

Merge Market Rush is designed as a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly ask players to provide names, addresses, phone numbers, account credentials, or other direct personal profile information inside the App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not ask you to enter:

  • Your real name
  • Your postal address
  • Your phone number
  • Your contacts
  • Your photos or videos
  • Your precise GPS location
  • Your payment card details

Payment details are handled by Google Play Billing and are not provided to us.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App may store gameplay and preference data locally on your device using Android app storage, DataStore, or similar local storage. This can include:

  • Campaign level progress and completed levels
  • In-game currency, stars, collections, and shop progress
  • Daily Order, Endless Rush, and reward state
  • Sound, haptic, and selected language settings
  • Whether the optional No Ads purchase is active
  • Consent status or privacy choices where required

This information is used to run the game, remember your settings, and restore your local play experience. Removing the App or clearing app data may delete local information unless platform backup or Google Play services restore it.

4. In-App Purchases

Merge Market Rush may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. The current purchase flow is for a non-consumable No Ads entitlement.

Google processes purchases and may collect information necessary to complete the transaction, such as your Google account, payment method, purchase history, device information, and fraud-prevention signals. We receive purchase status information from Google so the App can unlock or restore purchased content.

The No Ads entitlement is stored locally so the App can remember it on the device. When Google Play Billing is available, the App may query Google Play to confirm, restore, or reconcile the entitlement. No Ads disables interstitial advertising in the App. Rewarded ads may remain available because they are optional player rewards.

For details about how Google processes payment and purchase information, please review Google's privacy policies and Google Play terms.

5. Advertising

Merge Market Rush may show advertisements where ad services are configured and available. Ads may include interstitial ads between eligible level transitions and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a benefit. Rewarded ads are optional; if an ad is not available, the game continues without requiring you to watch one.

Advertising is provided through Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and may also be provided through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation with participating advertising networks such as Unity Ads when that runtime is configured. Advertising partners may collect or receive information from your device to provide, measure, limit, and improve ads. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and ad provider configuration, this may include:

  • Advertising ID or similar device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address
  • Device model, operating system, language, and app version
  • Ad impressions, clicks, errors, and performance signals
  • Consent status and privacy choices

We do not request precise location permission for advertising.

6. Consent and Privacy Choices

In regions where consent or privacy options are required, the App may display a form provided by Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). The availability and content of that form depend on your region, Google settings, applicable law, and the privacy message configured for the App.

If available, you can reopen privacy choices from the App settings. You can also manage ad personalization and reset or delete your Advertising ID through Android or Google settings.

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

The launch version of the App does not include a separate Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, or Onur-operated analytics SDK. The App may still receive basic technical information through Google Play, Android platform services, advertising SDKs, consent SDKs, or purchase systems. This information may help us understand crashes, purchase status, ad delivery, and compatibility issues.

If a dedicated analytics or crash-reporting SDK is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the store data disclosures should be updated.

8. Support Emails and Ad Reports

If you contact us by email, including through a "Report Ad" link, we may receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message content, screenshots, device details, app version, locale, approximate time of the issue, or ad information you provide.

We use support emails only to respond to your request, investigate issues, improve the App, and comply with legal obligations.

9. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing: Processing and restoring in-app purchases
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource): Ad mediation: selecting and delivering ads from participating networks
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads: Showing and measuring advertisements
  • Unity Ads: Showing and measuring advertisements
  • Google User Messaging Platform: Consent and privacy choice forms
  • Google Play services: Platform features, distribution, compatibility, and device services

These services may process information under their own privacy policies. Please review the relevant Google, Unity, and ironSource policies for more information.

10. Legal Bases and Regional Rights

Depending on your region, we or our partners may process information based on consent, legitimate interests, performance of a contract, legal obligations, or other lawful bases.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal information. Because the App does not maintain its own user account database, many requests may need to be handled through device settings, Google Play, Google advertising settings, or the relevant third-party provider.

You can contact us at onuragames@gmail.com for privacy questions.

11. Children's Privacy

Merge Market Rush is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to collect personal information from children. Advertising, consent, and store listing behavior may vary based on region, platform rules, rating settings, and provider policies.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

12. Data Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the App. Purchase records are retained by Google Play according to Google's policies. Support emails may be retained as long as needed to respond, maintain records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

13. International Processing

Third-party services used by the App may process information in countries other than your own. Those services are responsible for applying appropriate safeguards according to their own policies and applicable law.

14. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate for a small mobile game to reduce risks to information handled by the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide notice inside the App, on the store listing, or through another reasonable method.
